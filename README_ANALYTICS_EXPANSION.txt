FROZEN-EVIDENCE ANALYTICS EXPANSION
===================================

Purpose
-------

Build complete reporting-only analytics from the project's registered frozen
trade ledgers, equity paths, aligned benchmarks and existing robustness
artifacts.

The build does not run a strategy, optimization, walk-forward selection,
MCPT, bootstrap, paper simulation or market-data request. It never writes
inside results/EXP-001 through results/EXP-018 or data/.

Preflight (no output)
---------------------

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe build_analytics_expansion.py --preflight

Complete build
--------------

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe build_analytics_expansion.py

Open
----

results\analytics_expansion\index.html

The rebuilt research dashboard also exposes this index as "Complete
frozen-evidence analytics".

Output
------

- A root evidence manifest and cross-experiment summary.
- Separate EXP-001 through EXP-014 experiment reports.
- Separate analytics for all 134 registered strategy series.
- Explicit not-applicable pages for EXP-015 through EXP-018.
- JSON plus deterministic CSV tables for all applicable metric families.

Protection
----------

Every registered ledger, equity path, benchmark and frozen robustness
artifact is SHA-256 hashed before calculation, after calculation and after
output. A changed source aborts the build. Repeating a build with identical
evidence leaves every generated byte unchanged.

Full definitions and evidence boundaries are in:

research\ANALYTICS_EXPANSION_SPECIFICATION.md
